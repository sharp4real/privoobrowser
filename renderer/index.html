Before you start

A few things worth knowing.

Your browsing stays on this device. Privoo has no accounts, no sync servers and does not build a profile of you.

Privoo is built on Chromium. Where components such as yt-dlp or Widevine are used, their own licences apply.

Privoo is an independent open source project. It is not a company, and it is not affiliated with any browser vendor, tech company or government body.

Privoo comes with no warranty of any kind. The developers are not liable for data loss, privacy incidents or security problems. Use it lawfully and at your own risk.
