Before you start

A quick rundown — please read and agree to continue.

  • Privoo is provided as-is. It reduces tracking but is not a security guarantee. You're responsible for what you do with it.
  • Your data stays local. Privoo doesn't sync, profile, or send your browsing data anywhere. Everything is stored on this device.
  • Bundled engines may have terms of their own. Privoo uses Chromium for rendering and yt-dlp / Widevine when present — their licenses apply where they're used.
  • Use it lawfully. Don't use Privoo for fraud, harassment, or anything illegal where you live.
