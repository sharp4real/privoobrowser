Privacy and security

What the Privacy Shield does
The shield icon in the toolbar isn't one setting, it's a status display for the toggles below: ad & tracker blocking, Force HTTPS, third-party cookie blocking, Encrypted DNS, anti-fingerprinting, and WebRTC IP protection. Each one runs (or stops) independently and takes effect immediately when you flip it here, no restart needed, unless a toggle's own description says otherwise. Turning a protection off here means it's genuinely off, not just hidden from the shield panel.
Ad and tracker blocking
Block common ads and trackers. Takes effect immediately, in both directions.
Cryptojacking protection
Blocks known in-browser cryptomining scripts that hijack your CPU without asking. Applies after you restart Privoo.
Filter lists
Choose which built-in lists to use, or add your own. Changes apply after you restart Privoo.
Your lists
Force HTTPS
Upgrade insecure pages when possible.
Show HTTPS upgrade notice
Display a 3-second splash when Privoo upgrades a site to HTTPS.
Block third-party cookies
Reduce cross-site tracking.
Encrypted DNS (DNS-over-HTTPS)
Encrypts your DNS lookups end-to-end. Strict mode, never falls back to your ISP's plaintext DNS, so no leaks.
DNS provider
Cloudflare, 1.1.1.1, the default.
Custom DNS-over-HTTPS URL
Full https:// URL of your DoH endpoint, typically ends in /dns-query.
Block adult content (18+)
Routes DNS through a family-safe resolver (Cloudflare / AdGuard family filters) that blocks pornography and known malware domains. Requires Encrypted DNS to be on.
Do Not Track
Send the DNT request header.
Spoof user agent
Hides your real browser and OS signature from sites.
Canvas fingerprint protection
Adds subtle noise to canvas reads to prevent tracking.
WebRTC IP protection
Prevents WebRTC from leaking your real IP address.
Stronger tracking protection
Strips tracking URL parameters (utm_*, fbclid, etc.), minimises the Referer header on cross-origin requests, and sends the Global Privacy Control signal.

Site fixes

Some sites have aggressive bot detection that can flag privacy-focused browsers after a few failed attempts. Resetting their saved cookies + storage usually clears the flag without logging you out of anything else.

Reset TikTok login state
Fix for the "maximum number of attempts reached" error when signing into TikTok with Google.
Reset Discord login state
Use if Discord shows captcha loops or refuses to sign in after a few attempts.
Reset Snapchat web state
Snapchat's web client often refuses privacy browsers, this clears its session so you can sign back in.
Reset Reddit login state
Helps when Reddit keeps demanding a captcha or won't accept your password.
Reset Instagram web state
For Instagram's "suspicious activity" lockouts on a fresh browser.
Reset X / Twitter login state
Clears X's session if the login flow loops or rate-limits.
Reset YouTube / Google account state
Use cautiously, this signs you out of Google services in Privoo and clears Google's bot-detection flags.
Reset any other site
Type a domain (e.g. roblox.com) to wipe its cookies + site storage. Sub-domains are included automatically.
