Privacy Policy

Effective 25 August 2026

Privoo is built to keep your browsing data under your control. Most browser data stays on your device unless you choose to use an online service.

Data on your deviceConnected servicesProtectionYour choicesChanges

Data stored on your device

Privoo can store browser settings, bookmarks, browsing history, downloads, cookies and website storage, saved passwords, open-tab recovery data, and optional profile information locally on your computer. The exact data depends on the features you enable.

Private browsing uses a separate temporary session. Privoo is designed to clear that session’s browsing data when its private window closes. Downloads and files you explicitly save remain on your computer.

Websites and optional connected services

When you visit a website, your device communicates with that website in the normal way. The website may receive information such as your IP address, browser request headers, cookies it sets, and pages you request. Search engines, extension providers, wallpaper sources, media services and other services you choose to use have their own privacy policies.

If you configure Privoo AI with an online provider, the prompts and text you submit are sent to that provider to generate a response. Privoo’s attachment feature extracts text locally before sending it; it does not upload the original file as part of that feature.

Privacy tools and Privoo Guard

Ad and tracker blocking, URL-cleaning, fingerprinting protections and the Privacy Widget operate locally. The counts displayed by Privoo are local statistics, not a browsing profile shared with advertisers.

Privoo Guard is optional and uses ClamAV. If you choose to install or update it, Privoo downloads the scanner and virus definitions from ClamAV’s providers. Scan results are shown locally. No antivirus product can guarantee detection of every threat.

Your controls

You can clear browsing data, manage cookies and permissions, disable individual privacy features, remove optional downloaded components, and choose or remove a wallpaper in Settings. You can also avoid optional features such as AI, extensions and third-party web services.

Important: changing a Privoo setting changes Privoo’s behaviour; websites and third parties may still process information under their own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Privoo changes. The effective date at the top will be updated when that happens. Before a public commercial release, this policy should be reviewed and adapted by a qualified legal professional for the relevant operator, jurisdiction, data practices and contact details.
