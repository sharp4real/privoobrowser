Before you use Privoo AI

Privoo AI connects to third-party AI providers using your own API key. Please read the following before continuing.

  • Your messages are sent to the AI provider you choose (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, or Google). Privoo does not see or store them. Prefer to keep everything on-device? Pick Ollama to run a local model with no key and no data leaving your computer.
  • API usage is billed to your key. You are responsible for any costs charged by the provider.
  • Your API key is stored encrypted on this device only via your OS keychain. It is never uploaded or shared.
  • Do not share sensitive information such as passwords, personal data, or confidential content in your messages.
