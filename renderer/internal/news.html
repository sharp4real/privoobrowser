What's new

Privoo News

Every improvement, shipped straight to your browser. Rendered locally from this build — nothing is ever fetched, logged, or tracked.

Design · 5.0.7
A quieter, more considered interface

A full pass over the whole browser: one signature indigo accent instead of a scattered palette, calmer tabs and address bar with no bounce or glow, and popups (menu, downloads, shield, right-click) that finally look like part of the same app instead of a leftover skin. Selecting a colourful Theme still retunes the accent to match — it just isn't manually pickable any more.

Changed · Incognito
A private window that actually looks private

Incognito windows moved off the old purple-navy chrome onto a true near-black, and the "you're browsing privately" tab is a plain, calm page now, no glowing background, no boxed-in cards.

Improved · Transparency
Increase Transparency actually looks frosted now

The default glass style was blurring the desktop behind the toolbar by all of 3 pixels, barely visible. It's a real frosted-glass blur now, in both light and dark, with the mismatched blue tint on Liquid Glass dark mode fixed too.

Changed · New tab page
Adding a shortcut moved to Settings

The floating "+" button in the top-right of the new tab page is gone. Add, rename or remove shortcuts from Settings → Appearance → New tab page → Shortcuts instead — existing shortcuts on the page still open and remove exactly as before.

New · .mariana
Host your own anonymous site in one click

Open privoo://mariana, pick a folder with an index.html, and Privoo publishes it as a .mariana site served over Tor straight from your PC, no server, no domain, no port forwarding. Share the link and anyone can visit anonymously. When both people use Privoo, the page gets a second layer of post-quantum (ML-KEM-768) encryption on top of Tor. It's a real extra layer against future quantum attacks, and any Tor browser can still open the site over plain Tor.

New · Identities
Autofill that learns your details, not your data

Save one or more identities, name, address, phone, email, in Settings → Identities or privoo://identities, then right-click any form field and choose Autofill identity. Common fields fill instantly; a local Ollama model (nothing leaves your device) steps in only for unusual field labels the fast path can't recognise.

New · Decentralised web
.onion, .eth, and IPFS just work

Type a .onion address and Privoo brings up its own Tor circuit for it automatically, nothing else is rerouted. ENS (.eth) and Unstoppable Domains names resolve through a public gateway, and ipfs:// / ipns:// links open directly, no extension required.

Improved · Sidebar
Hover mode now truly gets out of the way

Set the shortcuts sidebar to Hover in Customize Privoo and it disappears completely, zero width, no leftover gap, until your cursor touches the left edge of the window.

New · Sidebar apps
Your apps, one hover away

Pin WhatsApp, Spotify, Discord, Instagram and more to the sidebar and open them in a proper mobile-sized panel, no more sites clipped to a tiny strip. Hovering an app that's playing audio now shows what's playing, with a pause button right there.

Fixed
Turning off ad & tracker blocking now actually stops it

The shield's ad-blocking engine only checked the setting once, at startup, so switching it off in Settings didn't do anything until you restarted. It's now checked live on every request, so the toggle takes effect immediately in both directions.

Fixed
New tab page polish

Cleaned up several small rendering glitches on the new tab search bar, a stray white outline that could show up over a custom wallpaper, quick-link text turning the wrong colour on hover, and a rendering seam that briefly appeared on new tab or hover.

Changed
System tray, back to the native menu

The tray icon's right-click menu is back to your OS's native context menu, now with more in it: new tab, new incognito window, downloads, history, bookmarks, settings, a minimize-to-tray toggle, and check for updates.

Rendered locally from this version of Privoo. No requests, no tracking, no telemetry.
