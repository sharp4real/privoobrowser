This release
Design
A calmer, lighter Privoo

A full visual refresh: light glass surfaces, a redesigned tab strip and search bar, and an accent colour that now runs through the entire interface — pick one in Settings → Appearance and everything from tabs to toggles follows it.

New — Sidebar apps
Your apps, one hover away

Pin WhatsApp, Spotify, Discord, Instagram and more to the sidebar and open them in a proper mobile-sized panel — no more sites clipped to a tiny strip. Hovering an app that's playing audio now shows what's playing, with a pause button right there.

Fixed
Spotify and TikTok no longer sign you out

Closing Privoo could drop the last few seconds of a site's login cookie, which mattered most for services that rotate their session constantly. Cookies are now flushed to disk the moment the browser closes, so logins actually stick.

Fixed
YouTube: no more black-frame videos

The very first video played each session could load to a black frame until you refreshed. That was a GPU rendering conflict — removed, so playback starts clean the first time.

DRM
Spotify and Netflix playback, properly signed

Widevine content protection is now correctly signed on every release, so DRM-protected audio and video play reliably instead of cutting out partway through.
