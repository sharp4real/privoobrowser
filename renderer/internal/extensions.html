Heads up. Some extensions may not fully work. Privoo runs on Electron, which implements most but not all Chrome extension APIs. Popups and content scripts usually work, but features that rely on contextMenus, privacy, webNavigation, or other advanced APIs may be limited or no-op. Privoo's built-in ad & tracker blocker already covers most of what blockers like uBlock Origin do.
Looking for an extension? Open the Chrome Web Store in Privoo and click Add to Privoo on any listing. It installs straight from there, no downloading required. The options above are for extensions that aren't on the store, or that you build yourself. Only install extensions from sources you trust.
