What's new
Privoo v4.0.0
Privoo for Android has released! 🎉

Privoo is now on your phone. Get Privoo for Android at privooapps.pages.dev — the same privacy-first browsing, in your pocket.

Privoo AI
Privoo AI, with chat history

The AI window and full-page view now have a chat-history sidebar — every conversation is saved so you can jump back in, just like a proper AI app. Open it from the AI button in the toolbar.

Privacy
Bring your own filter lists

In Settings → Privacy you can now add your own filter list URLs or switch off any of the built-in defaults (EasyList, EasyPrivacy, uBlock Origin…). Also new: keep Your Vibe off web pages, and a transparent search popup for vertical tabs.

Privoo v3.0.7
Profiles are here

Make separate profiles for work, home or family — each keeps its own history, logins, bookmarks and settings. Switch from the avatar in the toolbar or choose one at startup. Need a quick private session? Hit Browse as Guest.

Style & Discord
Make Privoo yours

Pick your interface font, corner style and write custom CSS in Settings → Layout. Plus: Privoo's accent now syncs with Discord automatically.

Make it yours
Customise everything

New tab styles, search-bar styles and glass looks like Liquid Glass in Settings → Layout. Plus browser sounds, a circular “+” button, and a draggable colour picker for your own theme.

Live wallpaper
Set a video as your wallpaper

Pick any video in Customize → Live wallpaper… — it plays muted behind your new tab and pauses when you look away to save battery.

Privacy
Proxy & Tor, built in

Route your traffic through a custom proxy, or launch Tor straight from Settings → Privacy for an extra layer of anonymity.

Sign-in
Smoother Google & TikTok logins

"This browser may not be secure" is gone — popup sign-ins now pass cleanly, and logging into TikTok with Google just works.

New tab
Clock, weather & more

Show a live clock and the weather on your new tab page — toggle them in Settings → New tab page and make it yours.

Vertical tabs
Polished side tabs

On a website, click the address bar to edit it in place; on a blank tab it opens the search popup. Closing the tab you're on is rock-solid now.

Languages
Browse in your language, anywhere

On a VPN in another country? Sites stay in your language now. Privoo follows your device locale — or pick any language in Settings → Privacy.
