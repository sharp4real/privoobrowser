What is a .mariana site?

A .mariana site is a website Privoo hosts straight from a folder on your PC, made reachable through the Tor network. There's no server to rent, no domain to buy, and no ports to open. Your computer is the host; Tor keeps both you and your visitors anonymous. It stays online only while Privoo is running.

On top of Tor's own encryption, Privoo adds a post-quantum layer (ML-KEM-768) whenever the visitor is also using Privoo: the two browsers run a post-quantum key exchange and encrypt the page a second time, end to end. This guards against an attacker recording traffic today to decrypt with a future quantum computer. It's a genuine extra layer, not a magic bullet, and anyone on a normal Tor browser can still open your site over plain Tor.
